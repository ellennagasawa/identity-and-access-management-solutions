    How to study & what this deck covers

    Try to recall the answer before flipping. Use Review again or I know this, then filter your cards to revisit the terms you need. Switch to Practice for matching, multiple choice, and short recall.

    On a flashcard, press Enter or Space to flip. Use ← and → to move through the deck when focus is outside a form field. All matching activities work with clicks or a keyboard; dragging is not required.

    This deck covers the eight supplied study guides for Section 2 of Introduction to Computer Networking – Beginner Crash Course, with introductory LAN and OSI context. Later lessons on subnet calculations, VLAN configuration, WAN/VPN, troubleshooting, security, and Wi-Fi configuration are outside this deck.

    Definitions are newly written. Course PDFs and slides are not included. Important corrections include HTTP at the application layer, changeable MAC addresses, and the difference between unknown-unicast flooding and broadcast. Read the source and correction notes.
